# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Nexus Skills to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives. Participation should be safe and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help newcomers participate.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's personal information.
- Disclose security concerns privately when public disclosure could create risk.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include harassment, threats, discriminatory language or conduct, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information without consent, sexualized attention or advances, and disruptive or bad-faith behavior.

Contributions that promote credential theft, malware, data exfiltration, evasion of security controls, or other harmful activity are not acceptable as project content.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, with the goal of protecting the community.

## Reporting

Report abusive behavior to the repository maintainer privately through a verified contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Include relevant links or context, but avoid forwarding private information beyond what is necessary.

Maintainers will review reports as fairly and confidentially as practical. The project does not promise a specific response time or outcome.
